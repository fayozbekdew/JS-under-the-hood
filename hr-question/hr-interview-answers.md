# HR / Behavioral Interview — Savollar va Namunaviy Javoblar (B1-B2 Ingliz tili)

> Har bir savolga oddiy, tushunarli ingliz tilida (B1-B2 daraja) namunaviy javob berilgan — murakkab so'zlar va idiomalar ishlatilmagan, shunchaki to'g'ri va tabiiy gaplar. Bu javoblar **shablon** — o'zingizning haqiqiy loyihalaringiz, kompaniya nomi va tajribangiz bilan almashtiring. Kvadrat qavs `[...]` ichidagi qismlarni albatta o'zingiznikiga o'zgartiring. Og'zaki suhbatda shu javoblarni asos qilib, o'z so'zlaringiz bilan gapirishga harakat qiling — yodlab olmang, tuzilishini (structure) o'rganing.

## 1. Tanishuv va o'zingiz haqingizda

**1. O'zingiz haqingizda qisqacha gapirib bering.**

Answer: My name is [ismingiz]. I am a frontend developer with [X] years of experience, and I mostly work with React and JavaScript. Right now, I am working on [loyiha turi, masalan: "a web application for online booking"]. Before that, I studied [yo'nalish] and I also completed some online courses about React. I enjoy building user interfaces that are fast and easy to use. In my free time, I like to learn new tools and practice my English.

**2. Sizni bir necha jumla bilan qanday tavsiflagan bo'lardingiz?**

Answer: I would say I am hardworking, curious, and easy to work with. I like to understand a problem fully before I start coding. I also like to ask questions when something is not clear, instead of guessing. My teammates usually say I am reliable and calm, even when there is a lot of work.

**3. Nima uchun dasturlash (yoki frontend/React) sohasini tanladingiz?**

Answer: I chose frontend development because I like to see the result of my work immediately. When I write code and see it change on the screen, it feels very satisfying. I also enjoy solving small, logical problems every day. React became my favorite tool because it helps me build interfaces in a clear and organized way.

**4. Rezyumeingizdagi eng muhim deb hisoblagan tajribangiz haqida gapirib bering.**

Answer: The most important experience for me was [loyiha yoki ish nomi], where I built [nima qilganingizni yozing, masalan: "the main dashboard for a small business app"]. I used React, and I also learned how to work with an API for the first time. This experience taught me how to plan my work and how to fix bugs step by step, instead of guessing.

**5. O'zingizning eng katta yutuqingiz nima deb o'ylaysiz?**

Answer: My biggest achievement was finishing [loyiha nomi] from start to end. It was not easy, because I had to learn new things during the project. But I finished it on time, and the final result worked well. I felt proud because I solved every problem by myself, step by step.

## 2. Kompaniya va lavozimga motivatsiya

**1. Nima uchun aynan shu kompaniyada ishlashni xohlaysiz?**

Answer: I want to work at [kompaniya nomi] because I like the products you build and the technology you use. I also read that your team works with React, which is my main skill. I think this company can help me grow as a developer, and I can also bring my knowledge and energy to the team.

**2. Nima uchun aynan shu lavozimga (rolga) mos kelasiz deb o'ylaysiz?**

Answer: I think I am a good match for this role because my main skills are [React, JavaScript va h.k.], and this is exactly what the job needs. I already have experience with [loyiha turi], so I understand how to build real features, not only small examples. I am also ready to learn quickly if there is something new.

**3. Bizning kompaniya haqida nimalarni bilasiz?**

Answer: I know that your company works on [kompaniya soha yoki mahsuloti, masalan: "web and mobile applications for different clients"]. I also saw that you use modern technologies like React, and you have a strong engineering culture. I really liked [biror aniq narsa, masalan: blog post, product, or review siz haqingizda o'qigan narsa].

**4. Sizni ushbu ishga qiziqtirgan narsa nima edi?**

Answer: What interested me most was the chance to work with a real product and a real team. In my previous experience, I mostly worked alone or on small projects. Here, I can learn from more experienced developers, and I can also work on something that many users will actually use.

**5. Nima uchun boshqa emas, aynan shu sohani (yoki texnologiyani) tanladingiz?**

Answer: I chose React because it is widely used, and it has a large community, so I can always find help when I have a problem. I also like how React organizes the code into small, reusable pieces called components. This makes the code easier to understand and easier to maintain.

## 3. Kuchli va zaif tomonlar

**1. Sizning eng katta kuchli tomoningiz nima?**

Answer: I think my biggest strength is that I don't give up when I face a difficult bug. I try different solutions, I read documentation, and I ask for help if I really cannot solve it alone. This helped me finish [loyiha nomi] even when I found some difficult problems in the middle of the project.

**2. Sizning eng katta zaif tomoningiz nima va uni qanday yaxshilashga harakat qilyapsiz?**

Answer: One area I am working on is time estimation. Sometimes I think a task will take less time than it actually does. To improve this, I now break big tasks into smaller steps, and I write down how much time each step takes. This helps me give better estimates now.

**3. Hamkasblaringiz sizni qanday tasvirlashadi deb o'ylaysiz?**

Answer: I think my teammates would say I am reliable and easy to talk to. If I promise to finish something, I usually finish it on time, or I tell them early if there is a problem. They also know they can ask me questions, and I will try to help if I can.

**4. O'zingizni boshqa nomzodlardan ajratib turadigan narsa nima?**

Answer: I think what makes me different is that I really care about clean, simple code, not just code that works. I always try to write code that other developers can read and understand easily. I also learn fast, so I don't need a lot of time to become useful in a new project.

## 4. Jamoada ishlash va nizolar (Behavioral/STAR)

**1. Jamoada qiyin odam bilan ishlashga to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering — bu holatni qanday hal qildingiz?**

Answer: In [loyiha nomi], I worked with a teammate who often disagreed with my ideas during code review. Instead of arguing, I asked him to explain his opinion in more detail, and I also explained why I made my choice. In the end, we found a solution that used the best parts of both ideas. This taught me that listening carefully can solve most conflicts.

**2. Hamkasbingiz bilan kelisha olmagan holatingiz haqida gapirib bering. Nizo qanday hal bo'ldi?**

Answer: Once, a colleague and I disagreed about how to structure a component. I thought my way was simpler, but he had good reasons for his way too. We decided to write both versions quickly and compare them together. After that, it was easy to choose the better option, because we had real examples, not just opinions.

**3. Jamoaviy loyihada sizning roli qanday bo'lgan va ishni qanday muvofiqlashtirgansiz?**

Answer: In [loyiha nomi], my role was to build the [masalan: "user profile and settings pages"]. I communicated with my team every day, usually in a short meeting, to share what I finished and what I was blocked on. When I needed something from another developer, like an API endpoint, I asked early so we did not lose time later.

**4. Kimningdir ishiga tanqidiy fikr bildirishga to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering.**

Answer: During a code review, I noticed that a teammate's code could cause a bug in some cases. I did not just say "this is wrong." Instead, I explained the exact situation where it could fail, and I suggested a possible fix. He appreciated the feedback because I focused on the code, not on him personally.

**5. Masofadan (remote) yoki turli vaqt zonalarida jamoa bilan ishlash tajribangiz bormi? Qanday muammolarga duch keldingiz?**

Answer: Yes, I have worked remotely on [loyiha nomi]. The biggest challenge was communication, because we could not always talk in real time. To solve this, I started writing clear messages with all the details, so my teammates could understand and answer even if we were not online at the same time.

**6. Sizga topshirilmagan, lekin jamoa uchun foydali bo'lgan biror ishni qilib qo'ygan holatingiz bormi?**

Answer: Yes, in [loyiha nomi], I noticed that our project did not have clear documentation for new developers. Nobody asked me to do it, but I wrote a short guide about the project structure and how to run it locally. Later, a new teammate told me it really helped him start faster.

## 5. Liderlik va tashabbuskorlik

**1. Boshqalarni yetaklashga (rasmiy lider bo'lmasangiz ham) to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering.**

Answer: In [loyiha nomi], I was not the official team lead, but I noticed that our team did not have a clear plan for a new feature. I organized a short meeting, and I helped the team split the work into smaller tasks. This made the work easier to track, and we finished the feature on time.

**2. So'ralmagan holda o'z tashabbusingiz bilan biror narsani yaxshilagan holatingiz haqida gapirib bering.**

Answer: I noticed that our project's build process was slow, and it made development harder. Nobody asked me to fix it, but I spent some time researching the problem, and I found a way to make the build faster. I shared this change with the team, and everyone was happy because it saved time every day.

**3. Qaror qabul qilish uchun yetarli ma'lumot bo'lmagan vaziyatda nima qilasiz?**

Answer: When I don't have enough information, I first try to find the missing information myself, for example by reading documentation or checking the code. If I still cannot find it, I ask the right person directly instead of guessing. I prefer to make a small decision and test it quickly, rather than waiting too long.

**4. Jamoadoshlaringizni motivatsiya qilishga to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering.**

Answer: During a difficult week in [loyiha nomi], the team felt tired because of a tight deadline. I tried to stay positive, and I offered to help with tasks that were not officially mine. I also reminded the team how much progress we already made. This helped everyone feel a little more confident about finishing on time.

## 6. Muvaffaqiyat, xato va bosim ostida ishlash

**1. Kasbiy faoliyatingizdagi eng katta muvaffaqiyatingiz haqida gapirib bering.**

Answer: My biggest success was completing [loyiha nomi] on my own, from the first line of code to the final result. I learned several new tools during this project, like [texnologiya nomi]. Seeing the finished product work correctly, with real users, was one of the best moments in my career so far.

**2. Ishda jiddiy xato qilgan holatingizni tasvirlab bering — undan qanday saboq oldingiz?**

Answer: Once, I pushed a change without testing it carefully enough, and it caused a small bug in production. I immediately told my team, and we fixed it together within an hour. After that, I learned to always test my changes more carefully before pushing, even small ones.

**3. Muddat (deadline) siqilib qolgan vaziyatda qanday ish tutasiz? Bunday holatga misol keltiring.**

Answer: When a deadline is close, I first list all remaining tasks and decide which ones are the most important. In [loyiha nomi], I had to finish a feature in two days. I focused only on the core functionality first, and I left small visual details for later. This way, we delivered a working feature on time.

**4. Bir vaqtning o'zida bir nechta vazifani bajarishga to'g'ri kelgan holatni qanday boshqargansiz?**

Answer: In [loyiha nomi], I had to work on two features at the same time. I made a simple list of tasks and decided which one was more urgent. I also communicated with my manager about my plan, so everyone knew what to expect. This helped me stay organized instead of feeling overwhelmed.

**5. Stressli vaziyatda o'zingizni qanday tutasiz? Real misol keltiring.**

Answer: When I feel stressed, I try to stop for a moment and think clearly instead of rushing. For example, during [loyiha nomi], something broke right before a demo. Instead of panicking, I quickly checked the error message, found the problem step by step, and fixed it in time. Staying calm helped me think more clearly.

**6. Kutilmagan o'zgarish yoki muammoga moslashishga to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering.**

Answer: In [loyiha nomi], the requirements changed suddenly in the middle of the project. At first, it was frustrating, because I already finished some work based on the old plan. But I quickly adapted, updated my code, and communicated with the team about the new timeline. In the end, we still delivered the project successfully.

## 7. Feedback va o'sish

**1. Sizga berilgan tanqidiy fikr-mulohaza (feedback)ni qanday qabul qilasiz? Misol keltiring.**

Answer: I try to see feedback as a way to improve, not as personal criticism. For example, a senior developer once told me that my components were too big and did too many things. At first, it was a little hard to hear, but I understood he was right, and now I always try to split my components into smaller pieces.

**2. O'zingizni doimiy rivojlantirish uchun nima qilyapsiz?**

Answer: I read documentation and articles about new features in React and JavaScript. I also try to build small side projects to practice new skills. Sometimes I watch tutorials or take short online courses when I want to learn a new topic in a structured way.

**3. So'nggi paytda o'rgangan yangi ko'nikma yoki texnologiya haqida gapirib bering.**

Answer: Recently, I learned more about [masalan: "TypeScript" yoki "React hooks"]. I started using it in a small personal project first, to understand how it works. Now I feel more confident using it, and I understand why it makes the code safer and easier to maintain.

**4. Sizga qattiq/qiyin feedback berishga to'g'ri kelgan holatni tasvirlab bering — buni qanday yetkazdingiz?**

Answer: I once had to tell a teammate that his code had a security problem. I did not want him to feel bad, so I explained the issue privately, and I focused only on the code, not on him. I also offered to help find a solution together. He thanked me later because I helped him fix it before it became a bigger problem.

## 8. Karyera maqsadlari

**1. 5 yildan keyin o'zingizni qayerda ko'rasiz?**

Answer: In five years, I see myself as a senior frontend developer with strong experience in React and modern web tools. I would like to work on bigger, more complex projects, and maybe help mentor junior developers. I also want to keep improving my English and communication skills.

**2. Qisqa muddatli va uzoq muddatli kasbiy maqsadlaringiz nima?**

Answer: In the short term, my goal is to get more experience working in a real team, with real users and real deadlines. In the long term, I want to become a strong, independent developer who can solve complex problems and also help guide other developers.

**3. Bu lavozim sizning karyera rejangizga qanday mos keladi?**

Answer: This position matches my career plan very well, because it gives me the chance to work with React in a real product environment. I can learn from experienced developers, and I can also grow my skills in a structured way, step by step.

**4. Kelajakda qaysi ko'nikma yoki texnologiyalarni chuqurroq o'rganishni xohlaysiz?**

Answer: I would like to learn more about TypeScript, testing, and performance optimization. I already understand the basics, but I want to become confident enough to use them in bigger, more complex projects without help.

## 9. Hozirgi/oldingi ish va bo'shliqlar

**1. Nima uchun hozirgi (yoki oldingi) ish joyingizdan ketmoqchisiz / ketdingiz?**

Answer: I am looking for a new opportunity because I want to grow more as a developer and work on more challenging projects. My current situation is good, but I feel I have learned most of what I can learn there, and I am ready for a new step.

**2. Oldingi ish joyingizdagi eng qiyin vazifa nima edi?**

Answer: The most difficult task was [vazifa nomi, masalan: "rebuilding an old part of the application with new technology"]. It was difficult because there was very little documentation, and I had to understand the old code by myself. In the end, I finished it successfully, and I learned a lot about reading unfamiliar code.

**3. Ish tarixingizda vaqtinchalik bo'shliq (gap) bo'lsa, buning sababini tushuntirib bera olasizmi?**

Answer: During that time, I focused on [sabab, masalan: "learning new technologies" yoki "personal reasons"]. Even though I was not working at a company, I stayed active by [misol: building projects, taking courses, or studying English]. I used that time to prepare myself better for the next opportunity.

## 10. Maosh va shartlar

**1. Kutayotgan maosh darajangiz qancha?**

Answer: Based on my experience and the market, I am expecting a salary around [summani yozing yoki oraliq]. But I am open to discuss this, because I also care about the team, the projects, and the growth opportunities at this company.

**2. Maosh bo'yicha muzokara olib borishga tayyormisiz?**

Answer: Yes, I am open to discussing the salary. I understand that the final number can depend on the full package, including benefits and growth opportunities, so I am happy to talk about it in detail.

**3. Ish haqidan tashqari, siz uchun qaysi shartlar (bonus, o'sish imkoniyati, ish grafigi va h.k.) muhim?**

Answer: Besides salary, growth opportunities are very important to me. I also value a flexible schedule and a supportive team where I can ask questions and learn from others. A clear path for career growth would also be a big plus for me.

**4. Bir nechta taklif orasida qanday tanlov qilasiz?**

Answer: If I had multiple offers, I would think about the team, the technology, and the growth opportunities, not only the salary. I would also try to imagine which company matches my values better and where I could learn the most in the next few years.

## 11. Ishga tayyorlik va logistika

**1. Qachondan ishni boshlashga tayyorsiz?**

Answer: I can start [aniq sana yoki muddat, masalan: "within two weeks" yoki "immediately"]. If needed, I am also flexible and can discuss the exact start date with you.

**2. Masofadan, ofisda yoki gibrid formatda ishlashga tayyormisiz?**

Answer: I am comfortable working in any of these formats — remote, office, or hybrid. I have experience with remote communication, and I also enjoy working with a team in person when possible.

**3. Ko'chib o'tish yoki xizmat safarlariga tayyormisiz (agar kerak bo'lsa)?**

Answer: Yes, I am open to that, depending on the specific situation. I would just need to discuss the details, like the location and duration, so I can plan my personal life around it.

## 12. Suhbatdoshga beriladigan savollar (yakuniy)

**1. Suhbat oxirida odatda so'raladigan "Sizda savol bormi?" degan savolga qanday javob tayyorlaysiz — qanday savollar berish maqsadga muvofiq?**

Answer: Yes, I have a few questions. Prepare 2-3 real questions before the interview — this shows genuine interest. For example: "What does a typical day look like for someone in this role?" or "What are the biggest challenges the team is facing right now?"

**2. Jamoaning kundalik ish jarayoni (workflow, code review, sprint) haqida qanday savol berardingiz?**

Answer: Could you tell me more about how the team works day to day? For example, do you use sprints, and how does the code review process usually work?

**3. Kompaniyaning o'sish va rivojlanish imkoniyatlari haqida qanday savol berardingiz?**

Answer: What growth opportunities are available for someone in this position? Are there any mentoring programs or paths to a senior role in the future?

**4. Muvaffaqiyat ushbu lavozimda qanday o'lchanishi haqida qanday savol berardingiz?**

Answer: How do you measure success in this role during the first few months? What would make you feel confident that a new hire is doing well?

**5. Jamoa madaniyati (team culture) haqida qanday savol berardingiz?**

Answer: How would you describe the team culture here? What do you personally enjoy most about working with this team?
